WHY ARE WE DOING THIS?

We are four Grade 8 students at Glamorgan School, working together on a toy-drive project for our community.

We chose this project because sharing toys is a simple way to bring people together, spread kindness, and help kids enjoy play. Our team plans and promotes the drive, and our school community can take part by bringing a toy to the collection bin.

OUR MISSION

Make it easy for our school community to donate toys and help more kids experience fun, creative play.

This website is one part of our Grade 8 project: it explains the toy drive and shows everyone how to take part.

WHY, HOW & WHERE?

Our Grade 8 project in three quick answers — and where every toy ends up.

WHY

WHY WE MADE THIS SLIDE

This presentation is part of our Grade 8 project. We made it to explain the toy drive, share our mission, and show our school exactly how to take part.

HOW

HOW WE DO IT

We plan together, build this website, and promote the drive around school — then collect, sort, and count every toy that lands in the bin.

WHERE

WHERE EVERYTHING GOES

The toys leave our office in boxes and head to the Calgary Firefighters Toy Association, a volunteer group giving toys to Calgary kids since the 1940s.
